🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard
1) Project Title
Pizza Sales Analysis – Retail Performance Dashboard

2) Objectives
- To analyze pizza sales data and uncover patterns in customer behavior, product performance, and time-based trends.
- To identify top-selling pizzas, peak sales hours, and category-wise revenue contributions.
- To support business decisions related to inventory, promotions, and staffing.

3) Tools Used
- Power BI– For building interactive visual dashboards.
- Pizza Sales Dataset – Includes order details, product categories, timestamps, and revenue data.

4) Key Insights
- Total Sales:
- Overall revenue reached 817.86K during the analysis period.
- Top Performers:
- Most ordered pizza: big_meat_s with 2K orders.
- Highest revenue generator: thai_ckn_l with 9.26K in sales.
- Sales Timing:
- Peak sales occurred between 12:00 pm – 2:00 pm.
- Lowest sales were recorded between 10:00 pm – 11:00 pm.
- Category Contribution:
- Supreme and Classic pizzas contributed the most to total sales.
- Day-wise Performance:
- Friday was the highest revenue day, followed by Saturday and Tuesday.
- Time Period Analysis:
- Afternoon generated the most revenue, followed by Evening and Night.

5) Conclusion
This dashboard provides a clear snapshot of pizza sales performance across products, time slots, and categories. It helps stakeholders understand customer preferences, optimize operations, and plan targeted promotions. The project demonstrates how data visualization can turn raw sales data into actionable business insights.
